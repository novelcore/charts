To get started with kubecore-operator:

1. Verify the operator is running:
   kubectl get pods -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. Check the CRDs are installed:
   kubectl get crd | grep kubecore.io

3. Verify Crossplane is installed (managed separately by kaos):
   kubectl get pods -n crossplane-system

4. Check Crossplane functions are installed (managed separately by kaos):
   kubectl get functions -n crossplane-system

5. Create your first KubeOrg resource:
   kubectl apply -f config/samples/schema_v1beta1_kubeorg.yaml

For more information, visit:
- GitHub: https://github.com/novelcore/kubecore-operator
- Documentation: https://github.com/novelcore/kubecore-operator/blob/main/README.md
